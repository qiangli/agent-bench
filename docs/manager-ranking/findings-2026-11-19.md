# Manager ranking — findings (2026-11-19)

**Question.** Which current models can manage a bashy sprint (conduct other
agents) or supervise sprint managers — and does the operator's experience
(Fable/Opus ahead of Astra/Sol) hold up against public data?

**Answer.** Yes. With every capability area weighted equally, public data ranks
Fable 5.2 and Opus 5.5 above GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol under both missing-data
methods — no weight tuning needed. Gemini 4 Carbon and Neon rank alongside
the anchors and are the models to certify next.

## Method

- **Data:** 451 rows from current public boards, 11 candidates from seven
  vendors, 11 capability areas (`agent-bench/data/benchmark-matrix-2026-11.tsv`,
  source URL per row). Math excluded; multimodal not measured; architecture and
  planning have no current public board.
- **Fit:** `yoke-seedfit --per-area` — item response theory per area (benchmark
  difficulty and discrimination; harness and source offsets absorb
  vendor-reported bias). Elo, dollar and index metrics are fitted through
  declared scales, not skipped. Config: `agent-bench/data/seedfit-manager.yaml`.
- **Missing data:** primary = missing cells skipped (the Epoch Capabilities
  Index approach), with same-line predecessor rows carried forward
  (`lineage-2026-11.tsv`, every inherited cell listed). Sensitivity =
  worst-case imputation (lowest observed − 0.05) on boards covering at least
  half the candidates. A conclusion is firm only when both agree.
- **Procedure (operator):** (1) rank with equal area weights; (2) the anchors
  Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5, GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol set the cutoff line (lowest anchor
  − 1.645·se); (3) choose the weights closest to equal that reproduce operator
  experience and rank everyone with them. Step 3 needed no tilt.
- **Hands-on is relative:** a model can manage a worker only when its coding &
  terminal θ − 1.645·se ≥ the worker's (nobody manages uphill, as
  `ladder.DominanceOK` for reviewers).

## Findings

1. **Operator experience confirmed.** Fable > Opus > Astra > Sol under both
   methods with equal weights. Fable and Opus are the only models that provably
   out-code Sol.
2. **Firm vs close.** Fable is clearly first. Opus over Astra is inside one
   standard error (0.08 primary, 0.02 worst-case) — consistent, not proven.
3. **Gemini 4 is the open question.** Carbon is 2nd under both methods. Neon is
   3rd (primary) or 6th (worst-case): it has no public orchestration or
   computer-use rows.
4. **The top tier cannot be separated on hands-on from public data.** Fable,
   Opus, Carbon, Neon, Astra and Sonnet sit within each other's uncertainty, so
   none provably out-codes another — peers, not a hierarchy, until host
   evidence decides.
5. **Weights barely matter.** Equal and role-weighted rankings differ only by
   adjacent swaps; the ranking comes from the data.
6. **Gaps no public board fills:** orchestration and code review (proxies
   only), steering *other agents*, architecture/planning, and agent-bench's
   missing `browser` pack.
